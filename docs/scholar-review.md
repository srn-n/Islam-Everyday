## I. Zakat (educational estimate)

- Disclaimer shown verbatim (English) with a Bangla translation. Formula unchanged: 2.5% of (cash + gold + silver + investments + business stock + money owed to you − debts due now) when that is at least the nisab; the lunar-year condition is explained, not calculated.
- Nisab weights offered: 87.48 g gold / 612.36 g silver (7.5 / 52.5 tola, default) or 85 g / 595 g (the weights Islamic Relief Australia uses). The user enters today's price; none is built in. Silver is the default basis.
- Sources: Sahih al-Bukhari 8 (Islam built on five, including zakat) as hadith; Islamic Relief Australia's zakat guide as charity guidance (nisab weights, hawl, debts due within 12 months, superannuation not counted until paid out). The two are labelled apart.
